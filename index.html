What I Do

Six things, done properly.

I don't do everything. I do the work that actually moves the needle for service-based businesses — and I do it myself, not via a junior or an offshore team. If you need a 40-person agency with a 4-month runway, I'm not your person. If you need one specialist who'll learn your business and own the outcome, keep reading.

  • 01 On-Page SEO Page-by-page optimisation of titles, structure, internal linking, and intent — the foundation everything else builds on. Core
  • 02 Local SEO Google Business Profile, map pack rankings, local citations, and geo-targeted landing pages for service-area businesses. Core
  • 03 Technical Audits Site speed, crawlability, schema, indexation. The plumbing that decides whether anything else you do gets credit. Core
  • 04 Backlink Acquisition Earned, contextually relevant links from real sites in your industry. No PBNs, no link schemes, no shortcuts that age badly. Growth
  • 05 AI Search Optimisation Structuring your site so ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews quote you instead of your competitors. Emerging
  • 06 Google Ads (Coming) Paid search to bridge the gap while SEO compounds. Currently in active study — available to existing clients by request. 2026
